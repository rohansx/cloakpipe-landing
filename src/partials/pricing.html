03 · Philosophy

Free to verify.
Paid to operate.

How the evidence works →

We do not charge more for better masking, and we do not cripple the free core to push an upgrade. Paid tiers add operations: hosting, evidence you do not have to run yourself, compliance packs and access control.

The open core is the whole product for one team. Detection and pseudonymization are commodities, so they are free and stay free. So is the evidence path: the ledger, anchoring with the CLI, release audit packs and the verifier. "Verify it yourself" only works if the verifier is unencumbered and anyone can run it.

Cloud is for running it across a company. Paying customers get the hosted release registry and promotion gate, sentinels, evaluation upload, hosted anchoring, auditor packs, the India DPDP pack, and, on Business, unmask RBAC and conduct policy. More regulatory packs, starting with RBI, are planned.

Pricing you can read

We publish indicative prices now and confirm them before Cloud is generally available. Self-hosting the open core has no per-call or per-gigabyte charge.

04 · FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Ask us anything →

What is free, what Cloud adds, and what is still on the roadmap. Anything else, email us directly.

Is the open source really free?

Yes. The core repository, rohansx/cloakpipe, is MIT licensed (the verifier crate is Apache-2.0). It includes the Rust proxy and CLI, the detection pipeline, deterministic pseudonymization and the encrypted vault, streaming rehydration, the MCP server, interceptor and tool gate, Agent Release manifests, signed certification and the GitHub Action, eval import, the evidence ledger, external anchoring with cloakpipe anchor, release audit packs and cloakpipe-verify. There is no usage cap and no per-call fee when you run it yourself.

When will Cloud be generally available?

CloakPipe Cloud is in early access with design partners now. We have not set a general availability date. Join the waitlist and we will reach out as places open.

What do design partners get?

Early access to Cloud, founding pricing, direct support from the founder, and a say in what we build next, including the regulatory packs. In return we ask for one real agent workflow and honest feedback. Email rohan@cloakpipe.co.

Can I self-host everything?

Everything in the open-source core, yes, with Docker or Compose, including fully offline. The Cloud features (the managed dashboard, hosted registry, hosted anchoring, auditor packs and the DPDP pack) are not open source and run as a managed service today. Running Cloud in your own VPC, on-prem or air-gapped is planned for Enterprise.

What is planned and what is available?

Anything without a Planned label works today. Planned items, such as the RBI pack, OpenTelemetry export, SSO, an SLA, VPC and on-prem deployment, customer-managed KMS, qualified-TSA anchoring and a TEE tier, are not built yet. SOC 2 and a HIPAA BAA come after our first paying customer. The changelog records what shipped and the docs state the limits of each feature.

Do you train on my data?

No. CloakPipe never trains on your prompts, responses or vault contents. When you self-host, detection runs on your infrastructure and nothing leaves it to be detected. The evidence ledger records entity types and actions, never raw sensitive values, so it is safe to anchor externally and hand to an auditor.
